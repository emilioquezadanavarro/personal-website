About me

I grew up in the 1990s watching Napster tear the music industry apart, the same industry I'd spend more than a decade working in.

Training as a musician taught me discipline and the patience to get things right. Later, as an Audio Engineer, my focus sharpened on something more specific: quality lives in the details you almost miss, and routing complex signal chains gave me an early intuition for how interconnected systems behave under pressure.

As an Operations Manager, I led multi-site teams, cut rollout implementation time by 50%, and reduced fault rates by 20%, work that gave me a real understanding of how businesses function and where they break.

Surrounded by the tools reshaping my industry, I made a deliberate decision to stop being the person who uses them and become the person who builds them.

I stepped away and reskilled in Software Development, AI Engineering and I am currently expanding my stack with AWS Cloud Engineering.

I'm now looking for a role where I can work across the full product pipeline, from idea validation to deployment.
