About me

I grew up in the 1990s watching Napster rewire the music industry, the same industry I'd spend more than a decade working in.

Training as a musician taught me discipline and the patience to get things right. Later, as an Audio Engineer, my focus sharpened on something more specific: quality lives in the details you almost miss, and routing complex signal chains gave me an early intuition for how interconnected systems behave under pressure.

As an Operations Manager, I managed three studio buildings and led their Site Managers, working with IT, Finance and HR to untangle the recurring failure points behind slow rollouts and inconsistent service. That work gave me a real understanding of how businesses function and where they break.

Surrounded by the tools reshaping my industry, I made a deliberate decision to stop being the person who uses them and become the person who builds them.

I stepped away and reskilled in Software Development, AI Engineering and AWS Cloud Engineering, and recently completed a 530-hour AI Engineer internship at Kudan GmbH, building a conversational agent for their PRISM Cloud spatial data platform.

I'm currently deepening my knowledge of JavaScript and learning to build with React to round out the front-end side of that pipeline.

I'm now looking for a role where I can work across the full product pipeline, from idea validation to deployment.

Based in Berlin. English (working language), German B1.2, improving towards B2 and decided to get a C1 certification.
